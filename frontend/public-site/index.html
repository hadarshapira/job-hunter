JobHunter

JobHunter is a personal job-search tracker built and used by one person, its owner, to manage a search for Senior Data Engineer roles in Israel.

It collects public job postings, grades how well each one fits the owner's CV, and tracks every application from “saved” to “offer”.

Why it asks for Gmail access

With the owner's permission, JobHunter checks the owner's own Gmail inbox for replies about job applications: confirmations that an application was received, rejections and interview invitations. It updates the status of the matching job and files confirmation and rejection emails under Gmail labels the owner chooses. It never sends, forwards or deletes email.

Private app

JobHunter is not a public service and is not open for sign-up. It runs on the owner's own computer, and its data is not shared with anyone.
