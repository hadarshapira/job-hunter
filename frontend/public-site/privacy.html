Privacy policy

JobHunter · Effective 16 November 2026

Who this app is for

JobHunter is a personal app with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else's data is collected.

What Google data it uses, and why

When the owner connects their Google account, JobHunter asks for permission to read and modify the owner's Gmail (scope gmail.modify). It uses this only to track the owner's job applications:

  • It reads new messages that arrive in the inbox to recognise emails about job applications: confirmations that an application was received, rejections and interview invitations.
  • It updates the status of the matching job in JobHunter, for example to “Applied”, “Rejected” or “Interview”.
  • It moves confirmation and rejection emails to Gmail labels the owner chooses, out of the inbox. Interview emails stay in the inbox.

JobHunter never sends, forwards or permanently deletes email, and it ignores messages that are not about job applications.

What is stored

  • For job-related emails only: the sender, subject, date, Gmail message ID and the action the app took.
  • The Google access key (refresh token), stored encrypted.

All data is stored on the owner's own computer. Full email contents are not kept.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. It isn't used for advertising, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

JobHunter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Gmail access can be removed at any time from JobHunter's settings, or from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. When access is removed, the stored access key is deleted.
